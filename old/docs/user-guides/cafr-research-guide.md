# CAFR Research User Guide

## Overview

The Trust Ledger System provides comprehensive tools for researching Comprehensive Annual Financial Reports (CAFRs) of municipalities and government entities. This guide explains how to search, analyze, and extract financial data from CAFRs for municipal bond due diligence and portfolio management.

## What is a CAFR?

A **Comprehensive Annual Financial Report** is the official annual report of a state or local government in the United States. CAFRs provide:

- **Financial Statements**: Balance sheets, income statements, cash flow
- **Revenue Details**: Tax collections, grants, fees
- **Expenditure Breakdown**: By department and program
- **Debt Information**: Outstanding bonds, debt ratios
- **Statistical Data**: Multi-year trends and demographics

CAFRs are essential for:
- Municipal bond analysis
- Credit risk assessment
- Investment due diligence
- Comparative municipality research

## Accessing CAFR Research

1. Navigate to **Research > CAFR Search** in the main menu
2. The CAFR research interface will open with search and analysis tools

## Step 1: Search for CAFRs

### Basic Search

Enter any of the following to find CAFRs:

- **Entity Name**: E.g., "City of Boston", "Los Angeles County"
- **State**: Two-letter state code (e.g., "CA", "NY")
- **Fiscal Year**: Four-digit year (e.g., "2023", "2024")

**Example Searches**:
- "City of San Francisco" → All SF CAFRs
- "Illinois" → All Illinois municipality CAFRs
- "2024" → All CAFRs for fiscal year 2024
- "City of Austin 2023" → Specific city and year

### Advanced Search

Click **"Advanced Search"** for detailed filtering:

- **Entity Type**: City, County, State, School District, Special District
- **Population Range**: Filter by municipality size
- **Credit Rating**: Filter by S&P/Moody's/Fitch ratings
- **Date Range**: Specific fiscal year range
- **Debt Level**: Filter by total outstanding debt

### Search Results

Results display:

- **Entity Name**: Official government name
- **Fiscal Year**: Report year
- **Report Date**: When CAFR was published
- **File Size**: PDF document size
- **Availability**: ✓ Available / ⚠ Limited / ✗ Unavailable

Click any result to view the full CAFR.

## Step 2: View CAFR Documents

### PDF Viewer

The integrated PDF viewer provides:

- **Full Document**: Complete CAFR with all sections
- **Navigation**: Table of contents and page jumps
- **Zoom Controls**: Adjust viewing size
- **Download**: Save PDF locally
- **Print**: Print specific sections

### CAFR Sections

CAFRs typically include:

1. **Introductory Section**
   - Letter of transmittal
   - Organizational chart
   - Certificate of Achievement

2. **Financial Section**
   - Independent auditor's report
   - Management's Discussion & Analysis (MD&A)
   - Basic financial statements
   - Required supplementary information

3. **Statistical Section**
   - Financial trends (10 years)
   - Revenue capacity information
   - Debt capacity information
   - Demographic and economic information
   - Operating information

### Quick Navigation

Use the **Quick Jump** menu to navigate to key sections:

- Statement of Net Position (Balance Sheet)
- Statement of Activities (Income Statement)
- Governmental Funds Financial Statements
- Proprietary Funds Financial Statements
- Notes to Financial Statements
- Statistical Section

## Step 3: Extract Financial Data

### Automatic Data Extraction

The system automatically extracts key metrics from CAFRs:

#### Revenue Analysis

- **Total Revenues**: All governmental revenue sources
- **Tax Revenues**: Property, sales, income taxes
- **Intergovernmental**: State and federal grants
- **Charges for Services**: User fees and charges
- **Other Revenues**: Investments, miscellaneous

**Displayed as**:
- Dollar amounts by category
- Percentage of total revenue
- Year-over-year change

#### Expenditure Analysis

- **Total Expenditures**: All governmental spending
- **By Function**: Public safety, education, health, etc.
- **By Department**: Department-level breakdown
- **Debt Service**: Principal and interest payments
- **Capital Outlay**: Infrastructure and equipment

**Displayed as**:
- Dollar amounts by category
- Percentage of total expenditures
- Trend analysis

#### Debt Metrics

- **Total Outstanding Debt**: All government debt
- **General Obligation Bonds**: Backed by taxing authority
- **Revenue Bonds**: Backed by specific revenue streams
- **Debt per Capita**: Total debt divided by population
- **Debt Service Ratio**: Debt payments as % of revenue

**Displayed as**:
- Current year amounts
- Historical trends
- Comparison to peer municipalities

### Financial Ratios

Automatically calculated ratios:

- **Debt-to-Revenue Ratio**: Indicates debt burden
- **Current Ratio**: Liquidity measure
- **Operating Margin**: Surplus/deficit percentage
- **Debt Service Coverage**: Ability to service debt
- **Fund Balance Ratio**: Reserve adequacy

## Step 4: Historical Comparison

### Year-over-Year Analysis

Compare CAFRs across multiple years:

1. Click **"Add Comparison Year"**
2. Select fiscal years to compare (up to 5 years)
3. View side-by-side comparison

**Comparison Views**:
- **Trend Charts**: Revenue, expenditure, debt over time
- **Growth Rates**: Annual percentage changes
- **Variance Analysis**: Identify significant changes
- **Ratio Trends**: Track financial health metrics

### Peer Comparison

Compare municipality to similar entities:

1. Click **"Compare to Peers"**
2. System identifies similar municipalities by:
   - Population size
   - Geographic region
   - Economic base
   - Entity type

3. View comparative metrics:
   - Revenue per capita
   - Debt per capita
   - Tax burden
   - Service levels

## Step 5: Link to Portfolio Holdings

### Associate CAFRs with Bonds

If you hold municipal bonds from this entity:

1. Click **"Link to Portfolio"**
2. Select bonds issued by this municipality
3. CAFR data automatically populates bond analysis

**Benefits**:
- Automated credit monitoring
- Alerts on financial deterioration
- Updated valuations based on CAFR metrics
- Streamlined due diligence

### Bond-Specific Analysis

For linked bonds, view:

- **Issuer Financial Health**: From CAFR data
- **Debt Service Coverage**: Revenue vs. debt payments
- **Revenue Trends**: Supporting bond repayment
- **Credit Risk Indicators**: Warning signs from financials

## Best Practices

### Research Workflow

1. **Start Broad**: Search by state or region
2. **Narrow Down**: Filter by population, credit rating
3. **Review Latest**: Always check most recent CAFR
4. **Compare History**: Look at 3-5 years of data
5. **Check Peers**: Benchmark against similar entities
6. **Monitor Trends**: Watch for deteriorating metrics

### Red Flags to Watch

Be alert for:

- **Declining Fund Balances**: Signals financial stress
- **Rising Debt-to-Revenue**: Increasing debt burden
- **Negative Operating Margins**: Consistent deficits
- **Pension Underfunding**: Long-term liability risk
- **Deferred Maintenance**: Infrastructure backlog
- **One-Time Revenue Sources**: Unsustainable budgets

### Due Diligence Checklist

For bond investments, review:

- [ ] Latest CAFR (within 12 months)
- [ ] 5-year revenue trends
- [ ] Debt service coverage ratio
- [ ] Fund balance adequacy
- [ ] Pension funding status
- [ ] Economic base diversity
- [ ] Management quality indicators
- [ ] Peer comparison metrics

## Troubleshooting

### CAFR Not Found

**Problem**: Search returns no results

**Solutions**:
- Verify entity name spelling
- Try alternate names (e.g., "Boston" vs "City of Boston")
- Check if CAFR published yet (typically 6-12 months after fiscal year end)
- Try broader search (state level)

### Data Extraction Issues

**Problem**: Financial data not extracted

**Solutions**:
- Some CAFRs may have non-standard formats
- Download PDF and review manually
- Use **"Request Manual Review"** for complex documents
- Check if CAFR is scanned image (OCR required)

### Slow Loading

**Problem**: CAFR takes long to load

**Solutions**:
- Large PDFs (>50MB) may take time
- Use **"Download"** for offline viewing
- Try during off-peak hours
- Check internet connection speed

## Understanding CAFR Terminology

### Common Terms

- **Fund Balance**: Equivalent to retained earnings in private sector
- **GFOA**: Government Finance Officers Association (sets standards)
- **GASB**: Governmental Accounting Standards Board
- **GAAP**: Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (government version)
- **CAFR**: Comprehensive Annual Financial Report
- **MD&A**: Management's Discussion & Analysis
- **RSI**: Required Supplementary Information
- **ACFR**: Annual Comprehensive Financial Report (same as CAFR)

### Fund Types

- **General Fund**: Primary operating fund
- **Special Revenue Funds**: Specific purpose revenues
- **Debt Service Funds**: Debt payment reserves
- **Capital Projects Funds**: Infrastructure projects
- **Enterprise Funds**: Business-type activities (utilities)

## Privacy and Data Use

- **Public Information**: CAFRs are public documents
- **No Confidential Data**: All information is publicly disclosed
- **Attribution**: Always cite CAFR source in reports
- **Updates**: CAFRs updated annually
- **Retention**: Historical CAFRs retained indefinitely
